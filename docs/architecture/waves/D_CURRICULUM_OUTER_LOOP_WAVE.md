# Wave D — Curriculum / Outer Loop

Status: **COMPLETE_FIRST_PASS**

## What changed

Wave D turns A (product context), B (English Domain) and C (canonical learner truth) into one explainable next-learning allocation.

The canonical path is now:

`Product Context + English Domain + Learner Truth`
→ prerequisite readiness
→ frontiers
→ allocation signals
→ TodayCurriculumPlan
→ LessonPlan
→ later E Teacher runtime

## Frontiers

- READY — prerequisite-ready new learning; unknown is not failure.
- REINFORCEMENT — fragile or support-dependent control.
- TRANSFER — local independent control exists but changed-context proof is missing.
- RETENTION — transfer exists but delayed proof is missing.
- BLOCKED — valuable but an unverified prerequisite prevents direct selection.
- MAINTENANCE — already supported through delayed independent evidence; prefer natural future opportunities.

## Policy boundaries

- Learner priority changes ranking but cannot bypass prerequisites.
- General and Exam share learner truth but receive different allocation signals.
- Exam deadline can increase urgency, but final scope/rubric/value policy belongs to S.
- General gets first-pass durable-output and transfer/retention signals, but final General policy belongs to R.
- 5/10/20/30/60 are time envelopes, never a fixed learner-facing staircase.
- D never chooses teaching mechanisms or writes evidence.

## Production consumption

`loadTodayCurriculumVMV1` is the production experience adapter. Today uses the canonical plan's time envelope and learner-friendly focus category. The deeper Today UX remains N's responsibility.

## Validation

Wave D tests cover prerequisite blocking/unlocking, reinforcement, transfer, delayed retention gate, learner priority, General vs Exam separation, coverage signals, time envelopes, Today planning, LessonPlan projection, and architecture handoff to E.
